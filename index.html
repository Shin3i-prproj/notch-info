Notch

A personal music notch for Windows

Notch is a small, private desktop app that sits at the top of the screen like a phone's notch. It plays music, shows synced lyrics, and can show your own YouTube Music likes and playlists after you sign in with your Google account.

It is a personal project for a single user. It is not distributed or offered to the public.
