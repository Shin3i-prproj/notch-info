Notch privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Notch is a personal desktop app for Windows, used by its author only. This page explains what it does with your data when you sign in with a Google account.

What Notch accesses

With your permission, Notch uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • read your YouTube channel name, your playlists and your liked music, to show them in the app;
  • like or unlike a song, only when you press the heart button.

Where your data is kept

Your sign-in (a Google access token) is stored only on your own computer, encrypted in Windows Credential Manager. Your playlists and likes are only held in the app's memory while it runs. Nothing is stored on any server run by Notch, because there is none.

Who your data is shared with

No one. Notch has no analytics, ads or tracking. It talks only to Google/YouTube (to use your account and play music) and to LRCLIB (sending a song's title and artist to find its lyrics).

Removing access

You can sign out in the app, which deletes the stored sign-in. You can also revoke Notch's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube API Services

Notch uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
